Privacy Policy for OmniConvert

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

Summary: OmniConvert is a 100% offline unit converter and tip calculator. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All calculations are executed purely in local memory.

1. Information Collection and Storage

OmniConvert adheres to a strict zero-data collection policy:

  • No Personal Information: We do not gather, record, or transmit any user data (names, emails, locations, or identifiers).

2. Device Permissions

OmniConvert does not request access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive device hardware.

3. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

OmniConvert contains no third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or data brokers.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via your Google Play listing contact email.
